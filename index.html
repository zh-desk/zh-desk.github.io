Sign in with your GitHub access key for the desk. It stays in this browser only.

How do I get a key?
  1. On github.com, click your profile picture (top right), then Settings.
  2. At the bottom of the left menu, click Developer settings, then Personal access tokens, then Fine-grained tokens.
  3. Click Generate new token. Name it ZH desk and pick an expiration.
  4. Under Resource owner, choose zh-desk.
  5. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories and pick desk.
  6. Under Permissions, set Contents and Issues to Read and write.
  7. Click Generate token, copy it, and paste it above.

Already signed in on another device? Open the desk there and use Other devices at the top to sign this one in with a QR code or a link.
