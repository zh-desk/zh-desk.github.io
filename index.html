Desk

Sign in with your GitHub access key for the desk. It stays in this browser only.

How do I get a key?
  1. On github.com, click your profile picture (top right), then Settings.
  2. At the bottom of the left menu, click Developer settings, then Personal access tokens, then Fine-grained tokens.
  3. Click Generate new token. Name it Desk and pick an expiration.
  4. Under Resource owner, choose zh-desk.
  5. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories and pick desk.
  6. Under Permissions, open Repository permissions. Set Contents and Issues to Read and write.
  7. Click Generate token, copy it, and paste it above.

Already signed in on another device? Open the desk there and use Other devices at the top to sign this one in with a QR code or a link.

    Sign in another device

    Your key works on any number of devices, but each browser needs it once.

    Phone or tablet

    Open the camera, point it at the code, and tap the link that appears. The desk opens signed in.

    Another computer

    Copy the sign-in link and open it on the other computer, or copy the key and paste it into the desk's sign-in box there. Saving the key in your password manager makes every future device one paste.

    The code, the link and the key each work like a password: anyone who has one can use the desk as you. Keep them to your own devices.
